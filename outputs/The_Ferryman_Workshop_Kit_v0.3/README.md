# The Ferryman v0.3 workshop kit

One complete station kit for facilitated paper testing, revision **workshop-1**, produced September 27, 2026. The [complete PDF](Print_and_Play_Workshop_v0.3.pdf) contains 36 A4 pages. Editable component data, layout scripts, local artwork, fonts, previews and validation evidence are included. No browser, account, hosting or installation is needed to use the printed kit.

Production defaults are **English, one station, A4, single-sided assembly, two starter cohorts** and pale text panels for ordinary home/office printing. These are practical assumptions, not confirmed workshop attendance, budget, date or duration. Existing v0.3 art is reused; no rules or balance values have been changed.

## Print and play

1. Print complete-PDF pages **1-30** for the starter station. Print pages **31-36** whenever another 12-person cohort is needed. Do not print the complete PDF and the section PDFs together unless extra copies are intended.
2. Choose **A4, actual size / 100%, single-sided**. Turn off Fit, Shrink and borderless enlargement. Measure the labeled 50 mm line before cutting. Keep uncut sheets at A4. Cards are 63 x 88 mm.
3. Cut the fine outlines. Sort souls C01-S01 through C01-S12, then C02-S01 through C02-S12. Further cohorts need fresh matching IDs on souls, partners, opponents and memory fronts.
4. Keep memory fronts in reserve until earned. Use an identical printed back and equal opaque stock or identical opaque sleeves for each earned memory. There are 32 alternatives but at most 24 earned memories from the starter souls. Never shuffle an unused alternative into play. Single-sided assembly is sufficient; duplex alignment is untested.
5. Lay out the board, three mats, resource sheet and references using the [station overview](previews/table-layout-overview.png). Keep event faces/trigger sheet with the facilitator unless the player explicitly chooses the spoiler reference. The overview itself shows event content.
6. Follow setup on page 24 and return/reset on page 25. Use pencils, erasers and optional erasable sheet sleeves. Extend waiting/wraith areas onto the table and reprint ledgers or use extra paper whenever needed. Finite pieces and printed rows do not cap the endless rules.

Physical printing, cut alignment, handling, table readability and a human rehearsal remain **NOT_RUN**. Complete the checklist in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) before a workshop. READY here means file/layout checks passed, not final art approval, proven balance or completed submission.
