## Reprint and reset

For each later cohort print pages 31-36, assign one new cohort number consistently to all fields, and append S01-S12 in order only when refill needs fresh supply. Do not recycle a delivered or active individual within a run. Reprint pages 17-18 for extra counters, 19-22 for extra bookkeeping, and 29-30 for new sessions. Keep continuation sheets and unused alternatives outside active piles.

Use page 25 to reset run state. A new run may reuse the physical starter cards after clearing all prior run marks. Optional knowledge notes may remain; no resources, souls, memories or mechanical unlocks carry over. Print at 100%, measure the 50 mm line and check a 63 x 88 mm card before cutting the full batch. All essentials are at least 3 mm inside card edges; page margins are at least 10 mm. Physical dimensions and opacity still need a printer check.
